# Hello, I'm Cooper Simpson

I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at the University of Washington in Seattle, where I started in 2024 and am advised by Alexsandr (Sasha) Aravkin. I am also a 2025 recipient of the Department of Energy Computational Science Graduate Fellowship (DOE CSGF). Broadly, my research interests are in computational and mathematical science. Primarily, my dissertation work is in complex large-scale optimization. I have worked in areas such as (non)convex optimization, deep learning, data compression, and algorithmic economics. I appreciate both the theory and application side of problems, which is to say I enjoy both proving a theorem and programming a software package.

Prior to starting my PhD I...
- Worked as a professional research assistant in the Aerospace Mechanics Research Center (AMReC) in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at the University of Colorado Boulder from 2022-2024, where I collaborated with Alireza Doostan and Stephen Becker on data compression techniques for large-scale scientific simulations
- Earned my B.S. and M.S. from CU Boulder in Applied Mathematics in 2020 and 2022 respectively, where I was advised by Stephen Becker for my masters thesis on non-convex Newton-type optimization methods
